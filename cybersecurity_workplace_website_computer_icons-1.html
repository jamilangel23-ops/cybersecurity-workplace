01 · INTRODUCTION

Why Cybersecurity Matters

Cybersecurity means protecting computers, accounts, networks, and information from unauthorized access, damage, or misuse. Data protection means handling personal and confidential information carefully and lawfully.

In the workplace

Offices use digital tools to communicate, store files, manage employee records, and serve customers. These activities need safe and responsible technology use.

Our goal

Help students and employees build safer digital habits, respect privacy, and understand that cybersecurity is everyone's responsibility—not only the IT team's.

02 · KNOW THE RISKS

Common Cybersecurity Threats

Knowing what to look for helps people respond before a problem becomes more serious.

🔑

Password attacks

Attempts to guess, steal, or misuse login details. Reusing passwords can put several accounts at risk.

🎣

Phishing

Fake emails, texts, or websites that try to trick people into sharing information or opening unsafe links.

🦠

Malware

Harmful software that may damage a device, disrupt work, or steal information.

🚨

Data breaches

Situations where information is accessed, disclosed, or exposed without authorization.

📲

Weak account security

Accounts can be more vulnerable when they lack strong passwords or an additional verification step.

📎

Unsafe attachments

Unexpected files may contain harmful content. Verify suspicious messages through a trusted channel.

03 · PRACTICAL STEPS

Everyday Cybersecurity Safety Tips

Make these habits part of your normal work routine.

🔒 Protect your accounts

  • Use long, unique passwords for each account.
  • Use an approved password manager when available.
  • Enable two-factor or multifactor authentication (2FA/MFA).
  • Never share passwords or verification codes.

Protect your devices

  • Install updates approved by your organization.
  • Lock your screen when you step away.
  • Use approved security tools and networks.
  • Back up important files according to workplace policy.

✉️ Spot suspicious messages

  • Check the sender and the context of the message.
  • Be careful with urgent requests and unexpected links.
  • Verify unusual requests using a trusted contact method.
  • Report suspicious messages to the IT or security team.

🤝 Be responsible online

  • Respect other people's privacy.
  • Use technology fairly and inclusively.
  • Do not access files without permission.
  • Follow your organization's cybersecurity policies.
04 · KEEP INFORMATION SAFE

Protecting Confidential Office Information

Employee records, customer details, financial documents, and internal files should be handled with care.

👤

Limit access

Share information only with people who are authorized and need it for their work.

📤

Check before sending

Confirm email recipients and attachments before sending confidential files.

🗄️

Store files safely

Use approved storage locations and follow company rules for keeping or disposing of records.

If you suspect a data leak or security incident: report it promptly through your workplace's approved process. Do not hide the incident or forward confidential information unnecessarily.
